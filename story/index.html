Pyrenees traverse · GR10, GR11, GR12 and HRP · 11–23 August 2026

Hendaye → Gavarnie

Thirteen days on foot along the French–Spanish border, from the Atlantic at Hendaye to the foot of the Cirque de Gavarnie. The plan was the Mediterranean at Banyuls. The knees ended it at Gavarnie, 301 km in.

04 · Looking back

What the 13 days add up to

Sources. Updates, timings and the two map pins come from the WhatsApp group export (11–23 August 2026). Distance, climb, descent, time, heart rate, steps and the GPS tracks come from the Garmin files behind your training dashboard (15 hiking recordings, read on 11 November 2026).

Gaps. Photos are from your iCloud shared album: 404 pictures resized to 1,100 px and re-encoded, so the copies carry no embedded location data; they are placed on the track by the time they were taken. The album's screenshots (tickets, a parcel label, app screens) and its one video are left out. Tracks are simplified to 80 points and 60 altitude samples a day. Garmin's moving time undercounts slow, steep ground. The 23 August heart-rate average (79 bpm) rests on 12 minutes of data. Temperatures are from the watch on the wrist and read warmer than the air.
